Moving around the map
• Drag with the left mouse button: move the map.
• Scroll: zoom in and out toward the middle of the view, at the height of the alerts (the track is drawn high above the map). Drag what you want to see to the middle first.
• Ctrl + drag (or Shift + drag, or drag with the right button): turn the view. Drag sideways to rotate around, up and down to tilt between looking straight down and looking from the side.
• Keyboard: arrows move, + / − zoom, Shift + arrows rotate and tilt.
• zoom to alerts flies to where the alerts are; first sign (when there is one) to the earliest steep altitude change; top view looks straight down (like a normal map); whole flight goes back to the start.
• Hover a point or line for details; hover the chart below the map to put a black dot on that moment.
• times (top): Israel time, UTC or your computer's time zone.
• The altitude slider stretches heights so climbs and dives are visible (×1 is true scale).
• show terrain draws the ground's hills and valleys (slower to load). Without it the map is flat, at the height of the airfield nearest the alerts.
• link to this view copies a link that opens the map as it is now. Link options: view=lat,lon,zoom,pitch,bearing,height (height in m, where the camera aims), x= altitude ×, terrain=1, go=alerts|first|top, zone=UTC|local, embed=1 (map only, for an iframe).
